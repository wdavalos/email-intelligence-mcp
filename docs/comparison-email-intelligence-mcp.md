## Key Differences

### AI Agent Integration
Email Intelligence MCP is built for AI agents using the MCP protocol. AI agents call tools like `find_email(name="John Smith", company="stripe.com")` directly — no API key management, no rate limit handling.

Hunter.io and Clearbit have REST APIs designed for developer integration, but require account setup, payment, and ongoing key management.

### Pricing Model
Email Intelligence MCP: pay per call with no monthly commitment. At $0.03/call, you get 1,600 email lookups for the price of one Hunter.io month — without a subscription.

### Data Sources
Email Intelligence MCP uses Hunter.io + Clearbit as data sources, combined with WHOIS and LinkedIn pattern detection for company email format inference.

## When to Choose Each

**Choose Email Intelligence MCP when:**
- You're an AI agent doing B2B sales research
- You need email finding + verification in one call
- You want pay-per-use without a subscription
- You're building an outreach automation product

**Choose Hunter.io when:**
- Your team does manual outreach research
- You need email verification alongside finding
- You want additional tools (domain search, etc.)

**Choose Clearbit when:**
- Your team does B2B data enrichment
- You need company and contact data alongside emails
- You have budget for premium data
